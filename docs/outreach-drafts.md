## Sending notes

- **Order of operations:** NGPF post first (it's the community hub and
  the warmest audience), Reddit a few days later, then start the email
  list. Don't post NGPF and Reddit the same day — if both take off you
  want bandwidth to answer comments fast; reply speed is the whole game
  on both platforms.
- **Personalize the first line of every email** ({state} mandate,
  a course name from the school site, a post of theirs). Two minutes
  per email; it's the difference between replies and silence.
- **Track every send** in the sales ledger in
  `b2b-classroom-packs.md` even if it's just "sent, no reply" — the
  follow-up email a week later ("just floating this back up — pilot
  offer stands") converts surprisingly well and is free.
- **The free pilot is the close everywhere.** It costs one env-var line
  and converts better than any discount.
